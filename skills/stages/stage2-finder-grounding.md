# Skill Pack: Stage 2 — Official Authoritative Grounding & Citation Verification

## Governing Authority
- Google AI for Developers (Search Grounding Specification), IETF RFC 9110, ECMA-334, OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025.

## Execution Directives
1. **Zero Unofficial Sources:** Reject and strip any reference to Medium, Reddit, StackOverflow, dev.to, Baeldung, DZone, Substack, personal blogs, or unverified forums.
2. **Mandatory Whitelisted Authorities:**
   - **.NET / C#:** `learn.microsoft.com`, ECMA-334 C# Language Specification.
   - **Java / Spring:** `docs.spring.io`, `docs.oracle.com`, `openjdk.org`, `jakarta.ee`.
   - **Python:** `docs.python.org`, `peps.python.org`, official FastAPI/Pydantic docs.
   - **Node.js / TypeScript:** `nodejs.org`, `typescriptlang.org`, `ecma-international.org`.
   - **DevOps / Cloud-Native:** `cncf.io`, `kubernetes.io`, `opentelemetry.io`, `cisecurity.org`, `owasp.org`.
   - **Regulated Domains:** WHO (`who.int`), AAP (`aap.org`), NICE (`nice.org.uk`), CDC (`cdc.gov`), PCI-DSS (`pcisecuritystandards.org`), ISO-20022 (`iso20022.org`).
3. **Normative Excerpt Precision:** Each `EvidenceBlock` must quote or summarize a specific technical rule and explain its direct application to the inspected files.
